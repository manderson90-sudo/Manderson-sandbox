ABOUT ME

A little about me.

I'm a technology leader based in the North East of England, with a career spanning financial services, professional football and retail. My experience has given me an appreciation of how technology, people and business performance come together.

MY JOURNEY

My career so far

My career began in retail, where I developed a strong foundation in operational management, customer experience and leading teams. Working in fast-paced environments taught me the importance of understanding how a business operates and the role technology plays in helping people perform at their best.

I went on to work in technology and systems management at American Golf, before joining Newcastle United Football Club in 2023. During my time at the Club, I was responsible for establishing and developing the Applications & Digital function, improving technology governance and introducing new ways for colleagues to access and use technology.

In 2026, I moved into financial services with Newcastle Building Society Group, bringing my experience in technology performance, supplier relationships and commercial management into a new sector.

Throughout my career, I've enjoyed the opportunity to build teams, improve processes, develop relationships and deliver changes that make a meaningful difference.

HOW I WORK

My approach to leadership

I believe effective leadership starts with understanding people, being approachable and creating an environment where teams feel supported and trusted.

01 / PEOPLE

People first

I enjoy developing people and helping them build confidence in their abilities. I believe in being approachable, listening carefully and adapting my leadership style to the needs of individuals and teams.

02 / COLLABORATION

Building relationships

Strong relationships are central to delivering successful outcomes. I place considerable value on working collaboratively with colleagues, stakeholders and suppliers to understand their challenges and priorities.

03 / IMPROVEMENT

Continuous improvement

I'm naturally curious about how things work and where they can be improved. I enjoy challenging existing processes, exploring opportunities and finding practical ways to make technology more effective.
