Zendesk Knowledge base and chatbot
01 / THE CHALLENGE

Making IT support easier to access

Before the introduction of the knowledge base, employees across Newcastle United Football Club were struggling to find the information they needed to resolve everyday IT issues.

The IT Service Desk was receiving a high volume of repetitive requests. Common enquiries included connecting guests to Wi-Fi, resolving printer problems and finding information about available hardware and services.

The team was spending valuable time responding to routine questions that colleagues could potentially resolve independently with access to clear, reliable guidance.

02 / MY ROLE

From idea to implementation

I identified the opportunity to use Zendesk's knowledge base capabilities to improve how colleagues accessed IT information and support.

I proposed the initiative, led the team responsible for designing and implementing the solution, and personally completed elements of the technical configuration.

My responsibilities included shaping the approach, overseeing delivery and ensuring the knowledge base supported the wider IT service catalogue.

03 / THE SOLUTION

What we delivered

01

A centralised knowledge base

We developed more than 100 knowledge base articles covering common IT issues, troubleshooting and available services.

Topics included printer troubleshooting, guest Wi-Fi, standard hardware specifications, peripherals available to order, email sending limits, email signatures and file sharing through OneDrive and SharePoint.

02

Service catalogue integration

We organised the knowledge base around the IT service catalogue, creating dedicated areas for different service categories, including collaboration and communication services.

This gave colleagues a clearer way to navigate the information available to them.

03

An integrated self-service chatbot

We introduced a chatbot that allowed colleagues to ask questions and receive answers drawn directly from the knowledge base.

The chatbot provided another route to information without requiring colleagues to contact the Service Desk for every routine enquiry.

04

Interactive guides using Scribe

We used Scribe to capture processes and create step-by-step guides.

These guides were embedded directly into knowledge base articles, helping colleagues follow instructions and complete common tasks independently.

05

A dedicated area for new starters

We created a dedicated section for new colleagues, bringing together useful IT information and guidance to help them get up to speed.

04 / INTERNAL BRANDING

Introducing nu:tech

The knowledge base was branded as nu:tech, aligning it with Newcastle United's existing internal platforms.

nu:tech IT knowledge and support
nu:voice Colleague intranet
nu:hub HR platform

The Club's Creative team developed the nu:tech name and visual identity.

The identity subsequently became the wider internal brand for IT services across Newcastle United.

05 / THE OUTCOME

Improving the employee experience

12% Improvement in customer satisfaction

Following implementation, we measured a 12% improvement in customer satisfaction (CSAT).

Feedback from internal customers was also positive, with colleagues commenting on how much quicker and easier it had become to find information and use self-service.

The knowledge base gave colleagues a central place to access IT guidance, while the chatbot made that information available through a question-and-answer experience.

For the IT team, the platform created an opportunity to reduce repetitive enquiries and spend more time on complex support requirements.
