PROJECT OVERVIEW

Establishing digital compliance within a growing leisure business

As American Golf expanded beyond its traditional retail business, I led the introduction of a digital compliance and inspection platform to support the launch and ongoing management of its new leisure operations.

Starting with food safety, the solution developed into a comprehensive operational inspection platform covering health and safety, facilities management, accident reporting and wider compliance activities.

The platform was subsequently adopted across the retail business, extending the benefits of the original implementation.

The project established consistent digital processes across geographically dispersed locations, improved the visibility and reliability of compliance records, and supported the integration of newly acquired leisure facilities.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Establishing operational standards within a new business division

American Golf had traditionally operated as a specialist golf retailer.

As part of its growth strategy, the business began expanding into leisure through the acquisition of golf courses, driving ranges, adventure golf facilities and associated food and beverage operations.

I was among the first employees transferred from the retail business to support the establishment of the new leisure division, helping to develop the systems and processes required to operate these facilities.

The business ultimately acquired six leisure sites across the UK, each bringing its own operational requirements.

With a relatively small central team supporting geographically dispersed locations, it was important to establish processes that could be monitored and managed remotely.

Food safety was an immediate priority. Working with an external food safety consultant, the business developed its Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP) arrangements to establish appropriate procedures.

I recognised an opportunity to introduce technology from the outset, creating a more consistent and auditable approach to recording and managing compliance.

I wanted to establish a digital approach that could scale alongside the business as further leisure sites were acquired.

02 / THE APPROACH

Introducing digital compliance from the outset

I personally recommended SafetyCulture as the platform to support the leisure division's operational compliance requirements.

My objective was to establish a consistent digital process that could be introduced across multiple sites, providing greater confidence in the accuracy, accessibility and completeness of compliance records.

The platform offered the ability to create structured inspection templates, capture supporting evidence and maintain digital records of completed activities.

Its centralised reporting capabilities also allowed operational managers to oversee compliance activities across geographically dispersed locations.

Those responsible for compliance could access records remotely and identify where further attention was required.

I led the implementation of the platform, developing the inspection templates and establishing the processes required for its use within the leisure business.

The initial focus was food safety, with the intention of extending the platform into other operational areas as the business developed.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION

Developing a repeatable approach across newly acquired sites

The six leisure facilities were acquired at different stages of American Golf's expansion.

The implementation was therefore delivered progressively, with SafetyCulture introduced as each new location joined the business.

I developed the digital inspection templates required to support food safety procedures, ensuring operational teams could complete and record their checks consistently.

The implementation also required consideration of the physical environment in which the technology would be used.

Working closely with Property Services, I coordinated the installation of electrical connections, network connectivity and Bouncepad tablet mounts in key kitchen locations.

This ensured the technology was readily accessible to colleagues carrying out their daily responsibilities.

The approach provided a repeatable implementation model that could be applied as additional sites were acquired.

As the leisure division expanded, the system scaled alongside it.

04 / REPORTING & GOVERNANCE

Improving visibility while maintaining operational accountability

A key objective was to provide greater confidence that compliance activities were being completed correctly across the leisure estate.

With sites distributed throughout the UK, it was impractical for the small central team to rely on regular physical visits to verify completed checks.

I configured the platform's dashboards, reporting and notification capabilities to provide operational managers with visibility of inspection activity.

Automated alerts helped direct attention to areas requiring action, while centralised reporting made it easier to review compliance information across different locations.

My responsibility was to establish the technology, reporting arrangements and supporting processes.

Day-to-day monitoring and operational accountability remained with the individuals responsible for food safety and site compliance.

This approach allowed the technology to support existing management responsibilities while providing a more structured and accessible source of compliance information.

05 / EXPANDING THE PLATFORM

From food safety to wider operational compliance

Although the original implementation focused on food safety, the success of the platform created opportunities to digitise additional operational processes.

I expanded the use of SafetyCulture to support a wider range of inspection and compliance requirements.

Food Safety & Hygiene

Digital food safety checks, cleaning records and supporting evidence aligned with the business's HACCP procedures.

Fire Safety

Structured fire safety inspections and associated compliance records.

Site & Facilities Inspections

Routine inspections supporting the maintenance and safe operation of leisure facilities.

Accident Reporting

Digital processes for recording incidents and maintaining supporting information.

Challenge 25

Recording activities associated with age verification and responsible alcohol sales.

Daily Operations

Opening and closing procedures, cleaning inspections and maintenance records.

The expansion demonstrated the flexibility of the platform and its ability to support different operational requirements within a single system.

Over time, SafetyCulture became an established inspection and compliance platform across the leisure division.

Its use also extended into American Golf's retail operations, broadening the value of the original implementation.

06 / TRAINING & ADOPTION

Helping colleagues demonstrate compliance with confidence

Introducing the technology was only one part of the implementation.

It was equally important that colleagues understood how to use the platform effectively and could confidently demonstrate their compliance activities during inspections.

I supported the training of operational teams, including how to retrieve and present historical inspection records to Environmental Health Officers.

One of the capabilities I particularly wanted colleagues to understand was the quality of the digital audit trail.

If an Environmental Health Officer visited a site, colleagues could select any date range and retrieve the inspections completed during that period.

They could open individual records and demonstrate the completed inspection, its date and time, supporting photographs and the signature of the colleague responsible.

This provided a clear and accessible record of compliance activities.

It also addressed some of the limitations associated with paper-based records, including the potential for documents to be misplaced or damaged.

By helping colleagues understand these capabilities, I wanted them to feel confident using the system during daily operations and external inspections.

IMPROVED AUDITABILITY

Accessible, evidence-based compliance records

Historical inspections, photographic evidence, timestamps and employee signatures could be retrieved quickly, helping teams demonstrate completed compliance activities during external inspections.

09 / REFLECTIONS

Building technology into the foundations of a growing business

Being involved in the establishment of American Golf's leisure division provided an opportunity to influence how processes were designed from the beginning.

By introducing digital compliance as part of the operational setup, we established a platform that could grow alongside the business.

The implementation reinforced the importance of understanding how technology will be used in practice.

From designing inspection templates and configuring reporting to coordinating physical installations and training colleagues, the success of the project depended on making the solution practical for the people using it every day.

What began as a food safety initiative developed into a wider operational compliance platform, supporting multiple business functions and eventually extending into retail.

It remains a project I'm particularly proud of because it demonstrates how a technology decision can create lasting improvements when supported by clear processes, effective implementation and continued development.
