Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Club-Wide AI Transformation

Leading the adoption of Microsoft 365 Copilot, from an initial pilot through to a Club-wide rollout, supported by a business case, employee training and responsible AI governance.

7,000 Estimated productivity hours saved following rollout
95% Regular usage achieved during the pilot
109 Users in the expanded pilot
01 / THE CHALLENGE

Making AI accessible across the Club

Newcastle United was progressing its digital transformation ambitions, with opportunities to improve how colleagues worked with information, documents, meetings and everyday administrative tasks.

Employees were increasingly exploring AI tools to help them work more efficiently. This created a need for an approved, secure solution that could support colleagues within the Microsoft 365 environment already used across the Club.

The opportunity was to introduce AI in a structured way, helping employees develop confidence and practical skills while supporting responsible use.

02 / MY ROLE

Leading the initiative

I initiated and led the Microsoft 365 Copilot pilot, overseeing its expansion as interest grew across departments.

My responsibilities included stakeholder engagement, licensing, adoption planning, financial analysis and developing the business case for Club-wide deployment.

Following approval, I oversaw the wider rollout and empowered a member of my team to lead the delivery of employee training workshops.

I also established an AI Champions network to encourage collaboration, share practical examples and support continued adoption across the Club.

03 / THE PILOT

Testing the value of Copilot

The initiative began with 25 volunteer employees and expanded organically as colleagues across the Club recognised opportunities to use Copilot in their day-to-day work.

The pilot grew to 109 users, with participation across departments including Football Analysis, Data & Insights, Commercial and Retail.

Usage reporting showed strong engagement, with regular adoption increasing from approximately 85% to 95%.

01

29,989 Copilot actions

Microsoft Copilot Impact Reports recorded 29,989 actions across the 109-user pilot during a six-month period.

02

Practical everyday use cases

Colleagues used Copilot to summarise email conversations and Teams meetings, draft documents, develop presentations, analyse information and find answers more efficiently.

03

Evidence-led evaluation

We used Microsoft reporting data to assess engagement and model potential productivity benefits, providing evidence to support the proposed wider rollout.

04 / BUSINESS CASE

Building the case for Club-wide adoption

Following the success of the pilot, I developed the business case for extending Microsoft 365 Copilot to desk-based employees across Newcastle United.

The proposal considered the financial investment, productivity opportunities, operational risks, employee experience and alignment with the Club's technology strategy.

I also prepared a return-on-investment analysis using actual Copilot activity data from the pilot.

We applied conservative, realistic and optimistic assumptions to estimate the time that could potentially be saved through wider adoption.

This provided senior stakeholders with a structured assessment of the expected benefits and helped support the decision to proceed with the rollout.

05 / IMPLEMENTATION

Delivering the Club-wide rollout

Following approval, we expanded Microsoft 365 Copilot beyond the pilot group to desk-based employees across the Club.

The rollout combined technology deployment, licensing management, employee engagement and practical training to support effective adoption.

01

Licensing and deployment

I oversaw licensing requirements and the wider deployment of Copilot across the Club's desk-based workforce.

02

Departmental training workshops

I empowered a member of my team to deliver practical training workshops, helping colleagues understand how Copilot could support their individual roles.

03

AI Champions network

We established a cross-departmental AI Champions network to share best practice, discuss new use cases and encourage continued learning.

04

Responsible AI adoption

The rollout was supported by the Club's mandatory Safe Use of AI training, reinforcing appropriate use of approved technology and awareness of information security.

05

Ongoing engagement

Internal communications, practical guidance and the AI Champions network supported colleagues as they developed confidence using Copilot.

06 / THE OUTCOME

Demonstrating the value of AI adoption

7,000 Estimated productivity hours saved following the Club-wide rollout

Following the wider rollout, our reporting indicated approximately 7,000 estimated productivity hours saved through the use of Microsoft 365 Copilot.

This estimate reflected the productivity benefits associated with Copilot usage and was separate from the initial pilot's financial modelling.

The project also established a structured approach to AI adoption, combining employee training, an AI Champions network and ongoing engagement.

It demonstrated how technology investment, supported by effective change management, could help colleagues work more efficiently across the organisation.
