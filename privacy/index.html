YOUR PRIVACY

Keeping things straightforward.

This is a personal professional portfolio. I aim to collect as little personal information as possible and be clear about how information is used.

This website, michaelanderson.uk, is independently owned and maintained by Michael Anderson.

I am responsible for the personal information I handle directly through this website and any enquiries I receive.

2. What information is collected?

You can browse this website without creating an account or providing personal details directly to me.

However, some information may be processed when you use the website or contact me.

Information you provide

If you contact me by email or through LinkedIn, I may receive information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your professional role or organisation, where provided.
  • The contents of your message.
  • Any additional information you choose to share.

Providing this information is voluntary. You do not need to contact me to use the website.

Technical information

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

GitHub may process technical information associated with website visits, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting services.

I do not require visitors to register or sign in to access the website.

3. How I use personal information

I use personal information only where there is a reasonable and appropriate purpose.

This may include:

  • Responding to messages and professional enquiries.
  • Discussing potential professional opportunities or collaborations.
  • Maintaining professional correspondence.
  • Reviewing requests concerning information published on this website.
  • Maintaining the security and operation of the website.

I do not sell personal information or use website enquiries to create marketing lists.

4. Lawful basis for using information

Where UK data protection law applies, I generally rely on legitimate interests when handling information submitted through professional enquiries.

These interests include responding to people who contact me, maintaining professional relationships, operating my portfolio and addressing requests about its content.

I consider the nature of the information and the reasonable expectations of the individuals concerned when using it.

Where information must be processed to comply with a legal obligation, that obligation may provide the relevant lawful basis.

5. Professional recommendations

This website features selected professional recommendations from people I've worked with throughout my career.

These recommendations may include an individual's name, professional job title, organisation, relationship to me and an excerpt from their recommendation.

The recommendations are based on professional feedback provided to me, including recommendations made available through LinkedIn.

I publish selected recommendations as part of my legitimate interest in presenting my professional experience and reputation.

If your recommendation appears on this website and you would like to discuss its presentation, request a correction or ask for its removal, please contact me directly.

6. Third-party services

I use third-party services to operate this website and communicate with visitors.

These include:

  • GitHub Pages: Website hosting and delivery.
  • Cloudflare: Domain and DNS management.
  • Apple iCloud Mail: Receiving and responding to email enquiries.
  • LinkedIn: Professional networking and direct messages.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy notices and applicable data protection arrangements.

Some providers may process or store information outside the United Kingdom. Where international transfers are relevant, applicable data protection safeguards must be in place.

You can contact me for further information about the services used in connection with this website.

7. Cookies and website analytics

This website is designed as a straightforward professional portfolio.

I have not added advertising trackers or analytics cookies to the website.

Technical services involved in delivering the website may process information necessary for operation, performance and security.

If I introduce optional analytics or other non-essential tracking technologies in future, I will update this notice and implement any required consent controls.

8. How long information is kept

I keep personal information for no longer than is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was received.

Professional correspondence may be retained while an enquiry or related discussion remains relevant. I review information and delete it when it is no longer needed, unless retention is necessary for legal reasons.

Professional recommendations may remain published while they are relevant to this portfolio, subject to appropriate review and requests from the individuals concerned.

Technical information held by third-party service providers is subject to their own applicable retention arrangements.

9. Keeping information secure

I take reasonable steps to protect personal information I receive from unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

The website uses hosting and communication services that provide security measures for their respective platforms.

As with any online service, complete security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights relating to your personal information.

These can include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Request deletion of information in certain circumstances.
  • Ask for restrictions on how information is used.
  • Object to certain processing of personal information.
  • Request data portability, where the legal conditions apply.

Your right to object

You have the right to object to processing of your personal information that is based on legitimate interests.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact me using the email address provided below.

I will consider requests in accordance with applicable data protection law.

11. External websites

This website contains links to external services, including LinkedIn and professional certification providers.

When you follow an external link, the destination website may collect and process information under its own privacy policy.

I recommend reviewing the privacy information provided by those organisations.

12. Automated decision-making

I do not use this website to make automated decisions about visitors or to profile individuals for marketing purposes.

The date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
